FreshGuard

A prototype of FreshGuard's expiration predictor. It looks at a small store's shelves and predicts which products will have food expire unsold tonight, so staff can pull it for donation in time. The store below is a synthetic example, and everything runs in your browser.

How the prediction works

A small store knows how many items were on each shelf in the morning and how many are left at closing, but not which ones. FreshGuard fills that gap by simulating many possible days. In each simulated day, shoppers usually take from the front of a column, sometimes reach behind, and staff occasionally restock and reshuffle. Days that end with the observed closing count are kept, and the leftover items are tallied by expiry date. The donation tracker and pickup planner, which use these predictions, are in development.
